# 🎂 Advanced Age Calculator

A modern and feature-rich **Advanced Age Calculator** built with **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**. This application accurately calculates your age and provides detailed information such as your zodiac sign, Chinese zodiac, day of birth, next birthday countdown, total time lived, and much more—all wrapped in a beautiful glassmorphism interface.

# 🎯 How to Use

1. Select your birth date.
2. Click **Calculate**.
3. View your:

- Exact Age
- Day of Birth
- Zodiac Sign
- Chinese Zodiac
- Age Category
- Next Birthday Countdown
- Total Days, Hours, and Minutes Lived

4. Use **Copy Result** to copy your details.
5. Use **Reset** to clear the calculator.
