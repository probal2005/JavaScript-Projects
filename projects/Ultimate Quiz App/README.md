# 🧠 Ultimate Quiz App

A modern, responsive, and interactive **Quiz Application** built using **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**. Test your knowledge with multiple-choice questions, track your progress, and view your final score with a beautiful animated interface.

---

## 📖 How to Play

1. Read the displayed question.
2. Select the correct answer.
3. Click **Next Question**.
4. Complete all questions.
5. View your score and accuracy.
6. Click **Restart Quiz** to play again.
